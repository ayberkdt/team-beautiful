## What it says [D-ag]
- **Opening:** the new Oostkamp facility opened on 11 Mar 2026. It is 6,000 m² and "doubles" production capacity.
- **Timing:** the opening fell on the eve of BEDEX, described as "the first international defence exhibition to be organised in Belgium".
- **Event:** invitation only. It included a technical demo of several systems and a tour, and ended with Belgian fries.
- **Jobs:** "up to 75 additional jobs in Oostkamp".
- **Partners:** described only as "national and international partners from the defence and technology sectors". **No partner is named.**
- **What Advionics does:** aerospace electronics, radar solutions, RF technology and integrated electronic systems, from design to maintenance.
- **Spokesperson:** **Luc Switten, CEO of Intersoft Electronics Group**, is quoted on doubling capacity and investing in talent and innovation.
