# S_advionics_testing: Advionics "Testing and Qualification" page

- **URL:** https://advionics.be/manufacturing/testing-and-qualification/
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (WebFetch)

## What the page says [D-ag]
- **Test levels:** boards, modules and complete systems.
- **RF test range:** "From DC up till 24 GHz".
- **Other test capabilities:**
  - flying probe testing
  - power supplies with remote-controlled loads
  - an in-house calibration department
- **Environmental screening:** temperature cycling, shock and vibration.
- **Pre-shipment:** every product gets "rigorous testing" before it ships.
- **Not mentioned:** MIL-STD, DO-160, EMC, anechoic chamber, near-field testing.

## Discrepancy
- This page says **24 GHz**.
- The Advionics product-development page says "DC to 26 GHz" (see S_advionics_product_development.md and R_advionics_research.md).
- **Use "up to ~24–26 GHz" or avoid quoting the number.**

## Relevance to us [Y]
- The jury expects qualification thinking: environmental screening and RF test to tens of GHz.
- Mention **qualification (DO-160 / MIL-STD-810)** for any hardware we propose. That standard choice is my suggestion; this page does not name either standard.
