Automated PAR Approach: interactive 3D simulation

Open this link in a desktop browser (Chrome or Edge):
https://sunumatik.vercel.app/sunum.html?k=aero&s=par_approach

What it shows
The simulation shows our automated Precision Approach Radar concept: Radar, Correction, Pilot Cue.
The aircraft flies a replay of our guidance model. The right panel separates what the pilot gets from what is only for the viewer.

How to use it
1. Click "Play approach". You can also drag the time bar.
2. Select a scenario:
   A Return: the aircraft starts off the path and returns to it.
   B Bad data: radar data is late, then invalid. Guidance is removed until good data returns.
   C Lost: radar contact is lost. The pilot gets the missed approach condition.
3. Click "Sound off" to hear the voice calls.

How to read the panel
Pilot will see: the flight display and the cue. The diamonds show where the path is. The small marker shows where the aircraft goes. The pilot puts the marker in the ring.
Pilot will hear: only the voice call.
Simulation only: everything else, including the 3D view, the map and the labels. The pilot does not see these.

Limits
Simulated data. Educational concept. Not approved for flight use.
The replay pilot and the aircraft are simple models. The flare and rollout are illustrative. The airfield is schematic.
The page needs a computer with WebGL and a graphics card. The same scenarios are also provided as videos.
