# E_08 — Ando et al. 2020: thermal structure from Venus Express + Akatsuki radio occultation

- **Citation:** Ando, H. et al. (2020), "Thermal structure of the Venusian atmosphere from the sub-cloud region to the
  mesosphere as observed by radio occultation", *Scientific Reports* 10:3448, doi:10.1038/s41598-020-59278-8.
- **URL read:** `https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7044293` (full text via WebFetch), accessed 2026-11-05.
  **[D]** for the text statements below (figures not digitised).
- Data: 280 Venus Express VeRa profiles (2006–2010) + 34 Akatsuki profiles (2016–2017), 1-km bins; open-loop retrievals
  reach ~40 km; nine 10° latitude bins; cloud layer quoted as 48–70 km (Introduction, Methods).
- Static stability S = dT/dz + g/cp: **0–1 K/km at 50–58 km at low latitudes** (low-stability layer); 1–4 K/km below
  50 km; > 4 K/km above 58 km (Results). The low-stability layer thickens toward ~70° and **reaches down to ~42 km
  poleward of 75°**.
- Temperature rises with latitude above ~65 km and falls with latitude below ~65 km; cold collar near 65° latitude and
  65 km altitude.
- Differences vs VIRA-2: up to 10 K at 0–30° above 70 km; up to ~5 K at 70–90° below 65 km (Fig. 4 text). Akatsuki is
  2–5 K colder than VEX at 45–75 km (0–30°). Venera 10 and PV profiles differ by 3–12 K.
- **Local time not analysed** (quote: "Dependencies on local time were not considered"). Random errors: VEX < 1 K,
  Akatsuki ~0.1 K; systematic errors not quantified.
- Numeric T(z, latitude) tables: only in figures — **not extracted**.

## Related, abstract only [A]
Tellmann et al. 2009, JGR 114, doi:10.1029/2008JE003204 (VeRa, 118 profiles Jul 2006–Jun 2007): ~30 K equator-to-pole
contrast at the 1 bar level; at cold-collar latitudes the tropopause is ~7 km higher and ~60 K colder; at 65 km VeRa
lies between VIRA and VIRA-2 and within ~5 K of PV Orbiter RO. Read only through the ESA abstract page
`https://sci.esa.int/web/venus-express/-/47293-tellmann-s-et-al` (search summary), not the paper.
