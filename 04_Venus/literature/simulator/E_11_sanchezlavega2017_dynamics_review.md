## Values [D]
- Abstract: zonal winds westward, nearly constant **~100 m/s at the cloud tops (65–70 km)** between ~50°N and 50°S,
  decreasing toward the poles; ~1–3 m/s in a ~10 km layer near the surface; meridional motions with peak ~15 m/s in the
  upper cloud at 65 km; vertical motions ~1–3 m/s in the statically unstable layer at ~50–55 km.
- Sect. 2.2: UV cloud tracking: ~−100 m/s between ±45°, decreasing poleward with meridional shear ~2.5×10⁻⁵ s⁻¹;
  long-term fluctuations ~10 %. At ~60 km (980 nm tracking): **mean constant velocity 70 m/s between ±45°**. At ~47 km
  (1.7 µm, night): similar pattern.
- Sect. 2.1: VEGA balloons at 53 km measured −69 ± 1 and −66 ± 1 m/s; below ~10 km u < 2 m/s; meridional v below
  60 km always < ±10 m/s.
- Sect. 2.3.1: PV probes: meridional v < 1–2 m/s below the clouds; **−5 to +5 m/s in the cloud region (above ~45 km)**.
  At 65–70 km meridional wind increases from 0 at the equator to ~10–15 m/s, peak near 60° latitude, poleward.
- Sect. 2.3.2: VEGA balloon vertical velocity fluctuated between −3 and +3 m/s (1 h ≈ 250 km of path); Venera descent
  data: w within ±1 m/s from 0 to 60 km; models: Hadley cell w ~0.1 m/s, waves/convection 0.5–5 m/s.
- Sect. 2.4: at 65–70 km, 10–40° latitude, zonal speed increases by **5–15 m/s from 07 h to 17 h local time** (solar
  tide); meridional v increases ~10 m/s at 14–16 h LT at 40–50°. **No local-time dependence detected at 60 km and 47 km.**
  Cloud-top zonal wind rose from ~−100 m/s (2006–2009) to ~−120 m/s (2009–2012) (Khatuntsev et al. 2013, as cited).
