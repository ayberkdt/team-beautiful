## Numbers [D-ag, manufacturer]
- Nominal capacity **13 Ah** (14 mA, +20 °C, 2.0 V cut-off); nominal voltage 3.6 V; OCV 3.67 V.
- **Nominal energy 47 Wh; typical weight 100 g** → stated **468 Wh/kg** and **65 W/kg**.
- Pulse up to 4 A (0.1 s); **max recommended continuous current 1.8 A** (~6.5 W).
- **Operating temperature −60 °C / +85 °C**; storage max recommended +30 °C.
- Self-discharge < 3 %/yr at +20 °C; hermetic glass-to-metal seal, stainless can.
- Dimensions Ø33.26 × 61.5 mm max.

## Relevance (mine)
Excellent energy and temperature range but ~65 W/kg power: a 300 W rotor load would need
≥ 4.6 kg of cells by power alone (300/65) — unsuitable as flight battery, suitable for
long-life housekeeping / beacon / survival power. A separate LSH 20-150 variant rated to
150 °C exists (search snippet only, 14 Ah, 104.5 g) [A].

## Caveats
Capacity strongly depends on current and temperature (datasheet curves at −40…+70 °C, not
transcribed). Primary: no recharge from solar.
