## Numbers [D-ag] (Soviet primary source, read in translation)
- Design entry condition: probe enters "at angle **−20 to −23°** to the local horizon"; landing on the illuminated side within lon 285–29?° (OCR), lat 15–3?°; probe–orbiter communication ≥ **115 min** (p. 6–7).
- Venera 9 probe, reconstructed from relay Doppler + onboard decelerations: entry 22 Oct 1975, **06:53:50 Moscow time**; **entry angle −20°30′**; landing **08:08:23** (Moscow); landing point 31°42′ (lat), 290°50′ (lon) (p. 11).
  - ⇒ [calc] entry-to-landing ≈ **74.5 min** for Venera 9.
- Descent described as "successive use of parachutes and aerodynamic decelerating panels" (p. 11); relative-velocity measurements continued ~3?0 minutes, of which 60 min after landing (OCR partial).
- Venera 10 entry values: not legible in the OCR text.

## Cross-checks
- Consistent with Venkatapathy & Smith 2016 range (−18, −23)° for Venera 9–Vega 2 (B_venkatapathy2016_prabhu2013).
- Siddiqi gives Venera 9 landing "05:13:07 UT" as Earth-reception time; Moscow = UT+3 → 08:13:07 Moscow received, vs 08:08:23 here (spacecraft time; ~4.7 min light-time difference plausible). Consistent.
