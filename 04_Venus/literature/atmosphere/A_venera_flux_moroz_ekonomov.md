## What I found

Only secondary statements, read in NASA-CR-169900 (Tomasko 1981, "The thermal balance of the lower atmosphere
of Venus", NTRS 19830011401, pp. 7–8) [D-ag]: Venera 8 measured downward flux 0.5–0.8 µm from 48 km to the
surface (~1 % of incident flux absorbed at the surface at SZA ~85°, Lacis 1975); Venera 9/10 measured up and
down flux 0.5–1.05 µm at SZA 28° and 33°; Venera 11/12 spectral measurements at SZA ~20°; ground reflectivity
at visible wavelengths < 15 % (Ekonomov et al. 1980). Net flux at cloud level ~15 % of up or down flux.
