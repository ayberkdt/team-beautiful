# E_06 — JPL Solar System Dynamics: Venus constants, 5 Nov 2026

- **Sources:** `https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/astro_par.html` (GM table, DE440) and
  `https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html`. Accessed 2026-11-05 via WebFetch (page text) **[D]**.
- GM Venus = **324858.592000 km³/s²** (DE440; no uncertainty printed on the page).
- Mean radius 6051.8 ± 1.0 km; mass 4.86731 ± 0.00023 ×10²⁴ kg; bulk density 5.243 g/cm³; sidereal rotation period
  −243.018 d (retrograde); equatorial gravity 8.87 m/s²; escape velocity 10.36 km/s.
- [K]: rotation rate 2π/(243.018 d) = 2.9925×10⁻⁷ rad/s; g(55 km) = GM/(6052.0 km + 55 km)² = 8.710 m/s²
  (6052.0 km = the Pioneer Venus altitude reference radius, E_01).
