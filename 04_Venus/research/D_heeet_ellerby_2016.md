## Numbers [D-ag]
- Design goal: peak heat flux ≫ 1500 W/cm², peak pressure ≫ 1.0 atm (100 kPa); scalable
  1 m to 3 m probes; target missions Saturn probe and Venus lander.
- Seam/gap-filler arcjet tests at **~7000 W/cm² and 5 atm** (IHF 3" nozzle).
- Venus reference probe for areal-mass study: 2750 kg, 3.5 m, 45° sphere-cone, nose
  radius 87.5 cm, β = 272 kg/m², V_inertial 10.8 and 11.6 km/s, EFPA −8.5 to −22°.
- **Venus heat load 12–36 kJ/cm²** (Saturn 75–250 kJ/cm²).
- HEEET 2-layer areal mass: potential > 40 % savings vs heritage carbon phenolic (zero
  margin, preliminary thermal model).

## Secondary (search-snippet only, not opened) [A]
- NASA fact sheet 2018: tested to ~6000 W/cm² and 5 atm; 30–40 % heat-shield mass
  reduction. phys.org 2018: 5000 W/cm², 5 atm. A project paper: design envelope
  1500–3600 W/cm², 1–5 atm; tests ~5000 W/cm² @ 5 atm and ~1850 W/cm² @ 14 atm; HEEET
  ~58 % of fully dense CP mass.

## Caveats
Presentations; areal masses are zero-margin. No Venus-specific kg/m² number read.
