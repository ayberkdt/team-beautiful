## Values [D]
- Model reproduces the convective layer position **46–55 km** and thickness **9 km** seen by VEX and Akatsuki RO
  (abstract/key points); stable regions above and below with a static-stability maximum near 63 km (Sect. 3.1).
- **Vertical wind about +2 m/s in updrafts and almost −3 m/s in downdrafts** at 51 km (Sect. 3.1, Fig. 4), consistent
  with VEGA −3.5 to +2 m/s.
- **Polygonal closed cells ~20 km wide** (Sect. 3.1); cell size scales with layer thickness.
- Predicts a 7-km-thick convective layer at the cloud top near 70 km (abstract).
- Gravity waves at 57 km: temperature perturbations −0.7 to +0.5 K (about 2× weaker than VeRa); horizontal wavelengths
  1–6 km without shear; with superrotation shear the waves reach the observed intensity (> 1 K) and horizontal
  wavelengths **up to 20 km** (abstract, Sect. 3.2).
- Variability: convective-layer thickness increases with latitude; little local-time change at the equator; the model
  reproduces Akatsuki's diurnal cycle of cloud convection at low latitudes (abstract, Sect. 3.3).
