## Numbers [A]
- Proposed vehicle: **15 kg** multirotor VTOL, able to lift ~60 % of its weight as payload (FlightGlobal).
- Carrier: a spherical Vega-type descent vehicle (mid-1980s Vega heritage). **Up to seven** multirotors could be deployed at once (Yatsenko, quoted by FlightGlobal; also MAI news per search snippet).
- Instruments mentioned: physical and chemical analysers, video cameras, ground-penetrating equipment.
- Presented at the 45th Korolev Readings. Context: Roscosmos Venera-D.
- The 2024 paper names mass and size criteria as the most important for choosing the layout (search abstract).
- Not found in what I read: rotor count, operating altitude, deployment altitude, endurance, power source.

## Use
- This is the only published, explicitly *multirotor* Venus concept I found. It is useful as a citation that the idea exists (Russian MAI, 2021-2024), but **the numbers need verification from the full papers before use in the report.**
