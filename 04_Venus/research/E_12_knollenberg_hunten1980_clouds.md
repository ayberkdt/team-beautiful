## Derived [K]
Mean mass loading = column mass / layer thickness: upper ~0.8 mg/m³ (13.5 km), **middle ~7.5 mg/m³** (6 km),
**lower ~21 mg/m³** (3 km). Text: "the dominance of mode 3 in the overall mass loading". Mode 3 is disputed by later
authors (secondary, search snippet [A]: Toon et al. 1984 suggested it is the tail of mode 2).

## Use
Droplet sizes and loading for (a) visuals (visibility, extinction ~ τ / thickness: middle cloud ~1.8 km⁻¹ → visual
range of order 1–2 km [K]), (b) leading-edge acid impingement, (c) direct-beam solar extinction.
