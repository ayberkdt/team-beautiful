[derived]: tip speed at 2800 rpm and R = 0.605 m is 177 m/s. That is M ≈ 0.75 at ~240 K in CO2 (a ≈ 237 m/s; my estimate, not from the paper). The paper's "≤ 0.6" therefore refers to the operating rpm rather than the 2800 rpm maximum. 360 W / 1.8 kg ≈ 200 W/kg of hover power at Mars density.

## Not in this paper (do not cite it for these)
Blade chord, solidity, the 2400-2700 rpm operating range, figure of merit, Reynolds number value, and survival temperature beyond "−100 °C or lower". See C_koning2018, C_grip2019 and C_ingenuity_jpl.

## Caveats
- Pre-flight design paper (Jan 2018). The flight article and flight record differ (see C_ingenuity_jpl).
- The ~90 s figure is a conservative worst-case end-of-mission estimate at 0 °C initial battery temperature (p. 15).
