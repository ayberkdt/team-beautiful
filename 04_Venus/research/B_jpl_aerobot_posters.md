## Numbers [D-ag]
- Task goal: altitude range **52–62 km**, ≥ **1 month**, stretch **100 Earth-days** (posters 1–3).
- Gondola design points **100–230 kg** (FY22) → **25–230 kg** (FY23/24).
- **Inflation on Venus "while hanging from a parachute" takes "approximately 10 minutes"**, outer (ZP) balloon filled with a slight head-start over inner (SP) (FY22 poster, "Inflation Testing"). Demonstrated with a subscale prototype in a hangar (high flow-rate inflations).
- Outdoor flights **July 2022**, ~**1/3 scale**, density equivalent **54–55 km** (FY22).
- Cruise packing: **zig-zag folded** packing demonstrated by Near Space Corp. (Phase II SBIR); multiple **extractions from packed state**, robust to lateral wind (FY23 "Packing/Inflation").
- Acid test: ZP material & seam after **96.2% H2SO4 at 100 °C under 63 N** tension — passed combined temperature-acid-load test, "meeting TRL5 requirement" (FY23, FY24).
- FY24: built **6.4 m** (objective text says 6.5 m) diameter ZP envelope with **3.5 m** SP reservoir — largest prototype; SP proof-loaded to 1 psi; SP seam goal **80 N/mm**; seam design +80% strength (optimized), +30% version used (FY24).
- TRL: aerobot & flight dynamics TRL 4–5; **deployment technology accepted as TRL 4** (FY24).
- Tethered-system poster (4): model of deployment/inflation assumes parachute fully open, system at terminal velocity, multiple viscoelastic tethers balloon↔gondola, variable-mass balloon; **Descent Rate Limiters** feed out the gondola tether to limit shock loads; simulated gondola deployment at floating altitude **57 km**; 1:4 scale lab rig; field tests summer 2023.
- Near Space Corporation renamed/listed as **Aerostar** by FY24.

## Relevance
Gives the only numeric aerial-inflation duration found so far for a modern Venus buoyant vehicle (~10 min under parachute). A rotorcraft deployed under a parachute would face the same "hang under chute, transition to free flight" phase but needs seconds-to-tens-of-seconds (rotor spin-up), not minutes.
