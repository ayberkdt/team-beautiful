## Extra Service Bulletin SB-NG-2-22, Issue A, 23.12.2022 "Canopy Frame Improvement" (7 pp., all read) [D]

- URL (public, manufacturer): https://extraaircraft.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/SB-NG-2-22A.pdf (copy downloaded by R1 to
  `D:\tmp_ideathon\easa\dl\reg\Extra_SB-NG-2-22A.pdf`).
- Header: EXTRA Aerobatic Aircraft GmbH, DOA **EASA.21J.593**, compliance **recommended**, no required compliance time,
  "at customer's discretion" (p. 1).
- **Applicability (p. 1): S/N NG001 through NG038, except for NG026.**
  - **Conflict with AAIB p. 12**, which says the SB applied to NG001–NG038 "which included G-MIIL (NG028) and SP-HMM
    (NG026)". As printed on the SB, NG026 (SP-HMM) is excluded.
  - Possible reason [Y]: SP-HMM's canopy was replaced or repaired at the factory after its 18 Mar 2022 event (PKBWL 2022/1097
    p. 10, "sent to the manufacturer for repair").
- Purpose (p. 1): improve the bonding between the inner and outer canopy frame "as a mere precautionary measure".
- Procedure (pp. 2–4): remove the canopy (AMM 53-20-01); drill three Ø 7 mm holes in the inner frame (430 mm left/right of
  centre); insert a packing-foam barrier at the front latch opening; inject 2-component PU foam; cure; reinstall.
- **Warning (p. 2):** too much foam may deform the frame or "**block the locking mechanism**". Step 15: check for free travel of
  the locking mechanism over its whole range.
- **Step 19 (p. 4), the clearest public description of the partial lock:**
  - "A released rear canopy handle will travel to a partially locked position where the shoot bolt lead wedge section is only
    engaged with the lug roller."
  - Full lock needs the handle pulled fully rearward to get audible and tactile confirmation. The lug roller then sits on the
    top flat of the shoot bolt (Fig. 2).
- Material (p. 6): beko 2-part PU framing foam or comparable; 1 man-hour warranty allowance. "New canopies already have
  improved bonding."
- Appendix A (p. 7): the return form asks whether any abnormality was found at the canopy glass, frame **or locking mechanism**.

**Use [Y]:**
- The SB itself can disturb latch travel. Our ICA therefore requires the LOCK-SENSE functional and rigging check after
  **any** canopy removal or frame work, not only at the 100 h / annual inspection.
- "Lead wedge only on the roller" vs "roller on the top flat" is exactly the threshold our bolt-depth sensor must resolve. The
  trip point sits on the transition from wedge to flat.

## FAA AD 2026-04-12, Amendment 39-23272 (final rule) [D]

- Federal Register Vol. 91, No. 51, 17 March 2026, Rules and Regulations (document 2026-05169), Docket FAA-2025-5386; PDF at
  https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-03-17/pdf/2026-05169.pdf (copy in `dl\reg\FR-2026-05169.pdf`).
- Summary:
  - Applies to all EXTRA NG.
  - Prompted by "reports of the canopy opening or detaching in flight because of a partially or improperly locked canopy".
  - Requires placard replacement and a POH/AFM limitations revision.
- **Effective 21 April 2026.** No comments were received on the NPRM; adopted as proposed (NPRM FR 2025-22445).
- (g)(1): within 3 months or 25 h TIS, replace the front and rear canopy placards per SB-NG-1-24 steps 1 and 2.
  (g)(2): before next flight, revise the POH/AFM limitations to include P/N EX-01106.01-Canopy. A further paragraph bans fitting
  any other placard from the effective date.
- **Costs:** 30 US-registered airplanes.
  - Placards: 1 work-hour × $85 + $10 parts = **$95 per airplane**, $2,850 US fleet.
  - POH/AFM revision: $85 per airplane, $2,550 US fleet.
- Use [Y]: the regulators' chosen fix costs about $180 per aircraft and adds no sensing. That frames our retrofit as the next
  barrier after placards and procedure, not a substitute for them.
