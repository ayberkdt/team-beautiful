## Header (AD p.1) [D-ag]
- AD No. 2025-0156, issued 21 July 2025, **effective 04 August 2025**.
- Design approval holder: EXTRA AEROBATIC AIRCRAFT GmbH. Type/model: EXTRA NG. TCDS: **EASA.A.620**.
- Manufacturer: Extra Flugzeugproduktions- und Vertriebs GmbH.
- ATA 11, "Placards and Markings – Modification / Aircraft Flight Manual – Amendment".
- Supersedure: none. Foreign AD: not applicable.
- **Applicability: EXTRA NG, all serial numbers.**
- Definitions (p.1):
  - "The SB" = Extra SB **SB-NG-1-24**.
  - "New canopy placards" = P/N **EX-01106.01-(X)**.
  - Group 1 = aircraft without the new placards. Group 2 = aircraft with them.
  - "Aeroplanes s/n **NG065 and up** had the new canopy placards installed in production before first flight."

## Reason (AD p.2) [D-ag]
- "Occurrences were reported of canopy opened or detached during flight."
- "in most cases, the root cause was a partially or improperly locked canopy."
- Consequences named: canopy separation, which could lead to damage to the aircraft structure, loss of control and/or injury to people on the ground.
- Extra's response, as the AD describes it: Extra "improved the design of the placards at both front and rear canopy locking handles", issued the SB for retrofit, and updated the POH/AFM Limitations section.
- The AD does NOT name any specific occurrence, registration or accident. It does not cite G-MIIL. [D-ag: no such reference anywhere in the 3 pages]

## Required actions and compliance times (AD p.2) [D-ag]
1. **(1) Modification, Group 1:** within **3 months or 25 flight hours, whichever occurs first**, replace the canopy placards at the front and rear canopy handles, as applicable, with the new placards per the SB.
2. **Note 1:** the pilot-owner may do actions (1) and (2) under M.A.803 / ML.A.803 of Reg. (EU) 1321/2014.
3. **(2) AFM update, Group 1:** before the next flight after (1), amend the AFM Limitations section to reference the new placards. Extra AFM/POH **revision 16** or later is an acceptable means.
4. **(3) Parts installation, Groups 1 and 2:** from the effective date, a replacement canopy may be installed only if it carries the new placard(s) and the AFM is updated per (2).
- Ref. publication: **Extra SB SB-NG-1-24 Issue A dated 19 December 2024**. Later approved revisions are acceptable.

## Facts for the team [Y]
- The regulatory fix for unlocked-canopy events on the Extra NG is **placards and an AFM change only**. No hardware lock change and no warning system are mandated. A canopy-unlocked alert retrofit therefore sits above the current mandatory baseline.
- The AD Reason frames the problem as a lock-state confirmation problem: partially or improperly locked. A sensor of full handle travel or shoot-bolt engagement targets that failure mode directly.
