## What the page says [D-ag]

**CNS systems**
- Air-to-ground and ground-to-ground **communication systems**.

**Airport**

- Flight data and flight plan systems.

**Meteorological systems**
- EASA- and DWD-compliant weather systems.
- AWOS.
- Runway ice detection.

**Consultancy and assessments**
- Expert assessments for airport construction projects.
- Obstacle and project compatibility studies.
- **RF impact analysis**.

**Radome services**

**Staff and markets**
- Work is done by "ATSEP technicians".
- Regions served: "Europe, the Americas and the Asia-Pacific region".

**What the page does not give**
- No customers or airports are named.
- No numbers.
- It does not say whether VCS, D-ATIS, MLAT and situation displays are Intersoft products or third-party systems that Intersoft installs. The wording reads like integration and maintenance services.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- This is the **only place in the group that touches voice and datalink**: VCS, synthetic D-ATIS with datalink, and aerial situation displays. These are offered as installation and maintenance services, not as own R&D products.
- A **voice → display** reading of the challenge (speech recognition of ATC voice feeding the controller display) is therefore adjacent to their service business, not their core RF products.
- If we pitch that reading, tie it to their strengths so it fits their world:
  - surveillance correlation, i.e. matching a spoken callsign to a Mode S/ADS-B track;
  - ASTERIX output;
  - SMS-style monitoring.
- MLAT appears here as an installed system, and SMS monitors it. Neither page shows that Intersoft makes its own MLAT product.
