## What it says [D-ag]
**Problem described**
- ADS-B networks combine sensors from different manufacturers, use different data formats, and feed several third-party applications.
- When ADS-B data is "fused into complex ATM systems", a badly performing sensor can be hidden inside the fused picture.

**Building blocks**
- **DHM**: acquires, routes, formats and records ADS-B and other CNS data.
- **MRD**: displays the ADS-B data.
- **SMS**: monitors ADS-B sensor status and performance live on the **SEAMP** (Sensor Alert Monitoring Panel), with automated reporting.
- Data can be passed to third parties "in any desirable data format".

**Systems the SMS can monitor**
- ADS-B
- PSR, SSR and Mode-S radars
- MLAT systems
- A-SMGCS and ASDE-X data
- **"ILS LOC & GP"** (ILS localizer and glide path)
- potentially other CNS systems

**Other**
- Intersoft Services can also supply, install and maintain the ADS-B sensors themselves.
- The leaflet gives no numbers.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- The same monitoring stack already covers the **ILS localizer and glide path** as well as surveillance sensors.
- One monitoring backbone spanning both surveillance and approach aids is how the sponsor sees the world.
- An approach-guidance concept with a **ground integrity monitor that feeds SMS** would therefore fit well.
