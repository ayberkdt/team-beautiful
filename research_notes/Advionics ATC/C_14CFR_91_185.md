## Content [D-ag]
- (a) Applies unless ATC authorizes otherwise, to IFR pilots who lose two-way radio communication.
- (b) **VFR conditions:** if the failure happens in VFR conditions, or VFR conditions are met later, continue VFR and "land as soon as practicable".
- (c)(1) **Route** (the "AVE-F" order): (i) **A**ssigned route in the last ATC clearance; (ii) if being radar **V**ectored, "the direct route from the point of radio failure" to the fix, route or airway in the vector clearance; (iii) the route ATC said to **E**xpect; (iv) the route **F**iled in the flight plan.
- (c)(2) **Altitude:** fly the **highest** of (i) the altitude or FL in the last clearance, (ii) the minimum IFR altitude for the segment, (iii) the altitude ATC said to expect. (Mnemonic "MEA", applied per route segment.)
- (c)(3) **Leaving the clearance limit:** (i) if the limit is the fix where an approach begins, start descent or approach as close as possible to the EFC time, or if none was received, to the ETA. (ii) If the limit is not that fix, leave at the EFC time (or on arrival over the fix if there is no EFC), proceed to the approach fix, and start descent near the ETA.
- The regulation gives **no fixed 7- or 20-minute hold** and **no 30-minute landing window**.
