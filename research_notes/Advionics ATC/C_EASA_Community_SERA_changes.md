# C_EASA_Community_SERA_changes: "Changes to SERA" (EASA Community post)

- **Issuing body:** EASA Community (Air Operations community). A post by John Franklin, so it is a summary and **not** legal text.
- **URL:** https://www.easa.europa.eu/community/topics/changes-sera
- **Post date:** 7 May 2025
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04

## Content [D-ag]
- Lists the rules touched by the 2025 SERA update: Art. 2 definitions; SERA.3212, 4005 (ballistic parachute information in the flight plan), 5005, 5010, 8015, 8020, "SERA.8035 and 14083" (RCF), 9010 (ATIS runway condition), 13015, 14090, 14100 (communicable disease).
- "From 1 May 2025, Article 5 has been removed." The supplement on ICAO differences now lives in separate compliance checklists on the EASA site.
- RCF timer: "a common time parameter of 20 minutes for both procedural and surveillance environment". Land within 30 minutes after ETA.
- States that SERA is aligned with ICAO Amendment 45 to Annex 2, Amendment 52 to Annex 11, PANS-ATM amendments and ICAO Doc 7030.

## Relevance to us [Y]
Independent EASA-side confirmation of the 20-minute timer, the 30-minute landing window and the 1 May 2025 date. It is only a summary, so cite C_EASA_SERA_EAR for the rule text.
