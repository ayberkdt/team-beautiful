# S_advionics_kongsberg_loi: Press release "Advionics and Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace Sign Letter of Intent…" (12 Mar 2026)

- **URL:** https://advionics.be/news/press-release-advionics-and-kongsberg-defence-aerospace-sign-letter-of-intent-for-ground-based-air-defence-cooperation-in-belgium/
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (WebFetch)

## What it says [D-ag]
- **Scope:** cooperation on current and future Belgian ground-based air defence programmes.
  - The companies will explore joint development and production of air defence systems and other technologies from Kongsberg's (KDA) portfolio.
  - They will also exchange technical expertise.
- **Terms:** the LoI runs for "three years", with an option to extend. It is **non-binding**.
- **Advionics' contribution:** "deep expertise in RF engineering, radar and antenna systems" plus "non rotating IFF technology".
- **People quoted:**
  - **Luc Switten, CEO of Advionics**
  - **Leif Roar Olsen**, SVP Air and Coastal Defence, Kongsberg Defence & Aerospace
- **Not in the release:**
  - NASAMS is not mentioned.
  - No contract values.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- Non-rotating IFF is Advionics' calling card, even toward air-defence primes.
- Here Luc Switten is styled CEO of Advionics. In the 11 Mar release he is CEO of the IE Group.
