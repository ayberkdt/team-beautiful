## What the page says [D-ag]
- **Platform:** ASR-M® with NGSP® processing.
- **Capabilities claimed:**
  - Adaptive clutter suppression for drones hidden in ground or urban clutter.
  - "High-resolution doppler processing to detect low speed and hovering targets".
  - Better track initiation and continuity on small manoeuvring drones.
  - "Integrated classification support to help differentiate drones from birds or benign air traffic".
- **Deployment options:**
  - Semi-fixed or integrated units.
  - "a dedicated channel for drone detection" can be added to an ASR-M airport radar.
- **Integration:** open architecture to connect with C2 systems, effectors and multisensor C-UAS frameworks.
- **Numbers:** none. The page gives no band, range, RCS or update rate.
- **Partnership:** a 13 Mar 2026 press release announces a partnership with EM&E Group and Indra Group to offer C-UAS in Belgium.
- **Linked whitepaper:** https://intersoft-electronics.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/IE-CD-01571-001-Advanced-Processing-Technologies-Whitepaper.pdf

## Relevance to us [Y]
- If our concept involves drones near the approach path (airport C-UAS, or drones flying approaches), the sponsor already has a drone channel on an airport radar.
- Reusing that channel to monitor the approach corridor would be a credible integration point.
