## What the page says [D-ag]
- **What it is:** an "L-band Integrated Electronic Scanning Array". It is a modular, non-rotating IFF antenna that can be "wrapped-around" the mast of a frigate. It uses a circular array, not multi-faced planar panels.
- **Modes:** IFF Modes 1, 2, 3/A, C, S, ADS-B, and Mode 5 Levels 1 and 2.
- **Levelling and multipath:**
  - "smart beam concepts for electronic levelling of rolling ship movements";
  - Vertical Antenna Pointing Diversity (VAPD), which gives electronic elevation beam steering and levelling;
  - it can "counter multipath nulling effects at flat sea".
- **Interrogators:** it can interface with any MSSR or STANAG/AIMS-certified IFF interrogator.
- **Scan modes:** continuous electronic rotation, confirmation lookbacks, and sector scanning.
- **Not stated on the page:** range, beam or element counts, update rate, weight, customers. These are in the leaflet; see S_intersoft_liesa_leaflet.md.
- **Leaflet link:** https://intersoft-electronics.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/IE-CD-01489-002-L-IESA-LEAFLET-A4-1.pdf

## Relevance to us [Y]
- Multipath nulling over a flat sea and roll levelling are exactly the issues a ship-deck approach aid faces.
- The jury has already solved them for IFF, so we should mention them explicitly.
