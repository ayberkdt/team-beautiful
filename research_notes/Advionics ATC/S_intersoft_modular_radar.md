## What the page says [D-ag]
- **NGSP®** (Next Generation System Platform) is made of "exciter, receiver and processor electronics". It integrates with an OEM Airport Surveillance Radar.
- The page states that "NGSP® is the system platform behind ASR-M®".
- **Modularity:**
  - The processor, receiver, transmitter and antenna systems are all modular.
  - Modules can be replaced and calibrated in the field "with minimal downtime".
- **Challenges it targets:**
  - air traffic density
  - wind farms
  - 4G/5G interference
  - low-RCS targets
- The page also offers monitoring and data analytics. It claims "unrivalled detection performance" but gives **no numbers**.
- **Customers:** none are named. ASR-M was nominated for "Product Made in Kempen".
