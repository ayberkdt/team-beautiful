# C_EUROCONTROL_Leaflet_Datalink_VHF: "Awareness campaign for pilots: Don't use the datalink channel for voice communication!"

- **Issuing body:** EUROCONTROL, Joint Datalink Performance Monitoring & Support Groups. Leaflet dated **01.04.22**.
- **URL:** https://ext.eurocontrol.int/WikiLink/images/3/32/Leaflet_VHF_radio_datalink_%282%29.pdf
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (PDF text extracted with pdftotext)

## Content [D-ag]
- Keep VHF channels **"(136.500 -137.000 MHz)"** reserved for datalink free of voice.
- EUROCONTROL found these frequencies "misused for voice communication causing a failure of datalink exchange". Pilots may think the channels are unused "because there is nothing audible".
- Data link reduces "the margin of error and misunderstandings in situations of poor voice connection" and frees "space on the congested VHF channels".
- "datalink frequencies are selected automatically by the avionics, voice frequencies are dialled in manually".

## Relevance to us [Y]
The top 500 kHz of the air band is digital. A receiver or decoder that only listens to audio would hear silence there. Any automatic tuning or "listen to everything" feature must exclude 136.500 to 137.000 MHz from voice use.
