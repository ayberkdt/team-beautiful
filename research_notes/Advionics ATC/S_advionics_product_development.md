## What the page says [D-ag]
**Engineering disciplines**
- "RF, analog, and digital electronics design", plus software and firmware.
- System engineering: architecture and requirements.
- Embedded software and hardware-software integration.
- Microprocessor circuits and programmable logic ("FPGAs and CPLDs").
- PCB design.
- Mechanical 3D CAD.

**Frequency range**
- Design: "5 MHz to 26 GHz".
- Testing: "DC to 26 GHz". Note that the testing page says 24 GHz (see S_advionics_testing.md).

**Test equipment**
- Network, spectrum and peak power analysers.
- Flying probe.
- Temperature, shock and vibration screening.
- In-house calibration.

**Certifications**
- ISO 9001, ISO 14001, EN 9100.
