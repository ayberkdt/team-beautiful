## What the page says [D-ag]
- **Radar types upgraded:** NGSP® has been used on "a wide range of different primary and combined radar systems". The page lists:
  - ASR / Terminal Approach
  - Gap Filler
  - **Precision Approach**
  - en-route
  - long-range 3D military radars
- **SSR:** NGSP can be "easily combined with Secondary Surveillance Radar (SSR)".
- **Why upgrade the processor:** processor electronics are "typically the first subsystem to become obsolete". NGSP is made up of an exciter, a receiver and processor electronics, and "Many modules can be replaced and reconfigured in-line without downtime".
- **Gaps:** the page names no customers and gives no numbers.
