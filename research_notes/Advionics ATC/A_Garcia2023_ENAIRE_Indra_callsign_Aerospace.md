## Snippet content [A]
- Callsign recognition rates: 84–87% for controllers and 49–67% for flight crew.
- Reason given: controller audio comes straight from the controller jack (low noise). Flight-crew audio is taken from the radio and its quality depends on factors such as distance from the receiving station.
- Proposed improvements: partial callsign recognition and richer phonetisation of airline names.
- Application: recognised callsigns are highlighted on the ATCo screen.
- Data: real-time simulation plus operational recordings (per title). Hours and SNR unknown.
