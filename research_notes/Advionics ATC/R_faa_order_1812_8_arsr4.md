## Facts, Par. 4b(1) "Radar Performance" (PDF p.2–3) [D-ag]
- Detection envelope: 0–360° azimuth; "5 to 250 nautical miles in range"; up to 100,000 ft MSL; −7° to 30° elevation.
- Scan rate: "12 seconds plus or minus 0.1 second".
- Range accuracy: ±0.125 NM (OCR shows "(113)"; probably 1/8). Azimuth accuracy: ±0.176°.
- Resolution: 0.125 NM in range at the same azimuth, or 2.0° in azimuth at the same range.
- Height accuracy (3-D radar): ±5,000 ft of true altitude, 90 % of the time, out to 175 NM.
- **Detection (Swerling I targets)**: 0.1 m² out to 92 NM; 1.0 m² out to 165 NM; 2.2 m² from 5 to 200 NM.
- Target definition: RCS 0.1 to 10,000 m²; velocity 25 to 3,000 kt; dynamics up to 7 g.
- Frequency: "1,215 to 1,400 megahertz" (L-band).
- Availability: unmanned, "operationally available 99.74 percent of the time" (PDF p.2). It must detect targets in ground, weather and angel clutter and minimise blind speeds.

## Beacon performance with the colocated ATCBI-5, Par. 4b(2) (PDF p.3) [D-ag]
- Probability of detection: 0.995, with round reliability 0.76, in 40,000 FRUIT replies per second (30,000 if operating Mode 4).
- Beacon range resolution 0.05 NM. Azimuth resolution 2.4° for identical codes.
- Code validation: Mode 2/3A > 97 %; Mode C > 96 %.
