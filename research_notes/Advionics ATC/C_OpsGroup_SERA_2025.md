## Content (as the blog reports it; the authority NOTAMs it cites were not opened by me) [D-ag for what the blog says; the underlying claims are not independently verified]
- Cites French NOTAM LFFF F0627/25, which refers to "IR SERA 2024/404", effective 1 May 2025, covering SERA.14083, the new code 7601 and the change from 7 to 20 minutes.
- Emergency descent wording changed: it now begins "Navigate as deemed appropriate by the pilot", replacing the old instruction to turn off route first.
- **Switzerland (non-EU):** NOTAM LSAS A0252/25 (15 May 2025 to 31 Jan 2026) requires 7600 even when continuing in VMC, and says 7601 is "not yet implemented".
- **UK (non-EU):** the CAA publication SN-2025005 is reported as making no change to UK RCF procedures.
- A reader comment says the SERA.8020 TAS tolerance changed from 5 % to 10 KTAS / M0.02. **[A] Not verified.**

## Relevance to us [Y]
The RCF rule set differs **by state** even inside Europe (EU 20 min plus 7601; CH 7600 only; UK unchanged). Any automated aid must take the applicable rule set from the AIP and NOTAMs for the airspace being flown, not from one hard-coded table.
