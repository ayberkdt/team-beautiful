## Content [D-ag]
- Art. 5 repeals **Regulation (EC) No 29/2009** (data link), **1206/2011**, **1207/2011** and **1079/2012** (8.33 kHz).
- Art. 2(3) defines "offset carrier operation": used where coverage "cannot be ensured by a single ground transmitter", with signals from two or more transmitters "offset from the nominal channel centre frequency". No offset-carrier requirement appears in the Annex.
- **Annex I Part-COM, Title 1 (data link):**
  - AUR.COM.2001: applies to IFR GAT **above FL 285** in SES airspace (excluding outside the ICAO EUR region, and the Finland and Sweden UIR north of 61°30′).
  - AUR.COM.2005(1)(a) required services: DLIC, ACM, ACL and **ATC Microphone Check (AMC)**.
  - AUR.COM.2005(2) exemptions include: CofA before **1 January 1995**; CofA before **1 January 2018** with ACARS-based data link; ≤ 19 seats and MTOM ≤ **45 359 kg (100 000 lb)** with CofA before **5 February 2020**; test, delivery or maintenance flights or MEL relief; and types in Appendices I and II.
  - Title 1 names **no** technology (no ATN/VDL Mode 2, ED-110B or ED-120 reference in the text returned).
- **Annex I Part-COM, Title 2 (voice):**
  - AUR.COM.3001: SES airspace in the ICAO EUR region where voice is provided in **117.975 to 137 MHz** (Canarias FIR/UIR excluded).
  - AUR.COM.3005(1): voice equipment put into service after **17 November 2013** "includes the 8,33 kHz channel spacing capability" and must also tune 25 kHz channels.
  - AUR.COM.3005(2): Art. 14(2) exemptions granted under 1079/2012 remain valid.
- **Annex II Part-SUR:** Mode S ELS for IFR GAT. ADS-B Out (1090 ES) and EHS for aircraft with MTOM **> 5 700 kg** or TAS **> 250 kt** and CofA on or after **7 June 1995**. Temporary inoperative transponder allowed for max **3 consecutive days** (AUR.SUR.2010).

## Relevance to us [Y]
This is the **current** EU equipage law (29/2009 and 1079/2012 are both gone). For the challenge: above FL 285 airliners must carry CPDLC (including the "microphone check" service against a stuck mic). Below FL 285, which is where **approach** happens, there is no EU data-link mandate, so approach communication is still voice. Any approach-guidance concept that relies on data link needs a voice fallback by design.
