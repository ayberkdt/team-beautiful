# C_EU_1079_2012: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) No 1079/2012, voice channel spacing (8.33 kHz) for the single European sky

- **Issuing body:** European Commission. Text read on legislation.gov.uk in the **"as adopted"** (original EU) version, because EUR-Lex returned empty pages to the fetch tool.
- **URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2012/1079/adopted/data.htm?view=plain
- **Adopted:** 16 November 2012
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04
- **STATUS WARNING:** this regulation was **repealed** by Implementing Regulation (EU) 2023/1770, Art. 5 (see C_EU_2023_1770). Its dates are historical. The surviving obligation is now AUR.COM.3005.

## Content [D-ag]
- Art. 1: "coordinated introduction of air-ground voice communications based on 8,33 kHz channel spacing".
- Art. 2(1) scope: radios operating in the **"117,975-137 MHz band"**.
- Art. 3(2): 8.33 kHz spacing means "nominal channel centre frequencies are separated in increments of 8,33 kHz". Art. 3(1): a "channel" is the tuning designator that "allows unique identification of the applicable radio frequency and associated channel spacing".
- Art. 4(1)-(4): **"from 17 November 2013"**, radios placed on the market or put into service, new aircraft and upgraded radios must be 8.33-capable.
- Art. 4(5): **"by 31 December 2017 at the latest"**, all radios must be 8.33-capable (except ANSP ground radios).
- Art. 5(2): **"From 1 January 2014"**, IFR in airspace classes A, B and C of the Annex I states. Art. 5(4): **"from 1 January 2018"**, operators must have the capability wherever carriage of a radio is required.
- Art. 6(3): at least **25 %** of 25 kHz ACC assignments converted "by 31 December 2014 at the latest". Art. 6(8): operational control frequencies 8.33 "from 1 January 2015". Art. 6(10): all assignments converted **"by 31 December 2018 at the latest"**, except those kept at 25 kHz for safety reasons or State aircraft.
- Art. 9: State aircraft dates (e.g. 9(8) "by 31 December 2018 at the latest", 9(10) no later than 31 December 2020).
- Art. 14: Member States may grant local exemptions from 4(5), 5(4) and 6(10).

## Relevance to us [Y]
The European VHF voice band is 117.975 to 137 MHz, and the 8.33 kHz grid triples the channels in each 25 kHz slot. A "translate signal" or display concept has to show 6-digit **channel names** (e.g. 118.005), not raw frequencies. These names are what the pilot hears and dials (C_EASA_SERA_EAR §5, C_ETSI_EN300676_1).
