## Content [D-ag]
- Scope: VDL Mode 2 air interface in the VHF band (117,975 MHz to 137,000 MHz), "Differential Eight Phase Shift Keying (D8PSK), intended for channel increments of 25 kHz". The layer diagram shows "D8PSK (31,5 kbits/s) Layer 1".
- Clause 5.6: tunes to "any of the **760 channels** of 25 kHz width from 118,000 MHz to 136,975 MHz".
- Clause 6.1: maximum burst length **4,16 s** at the nominal symbol rate.
- Clause 6.1.6: "The Mode 2 symbol rate shall be **10 500 symbols/s ± 50 ppm**, resulting in a nominal bit rate of **31 500 bits/s**".
- Clause 6.2: uncorrected BER requirement **10-3** (i.e. 10^-3). Reference signal level **-87 dBm**.
- Clause 6.2.1: sensitivity, a signal of max **-98 dBm** must give that BER.
- Adjacent channel rejection must be ≥ 44 dB.
- (The common signalling channel 136.975 MHz is **not** stated in the lines I extracted. See C_A_unverified_snippets.)

## Relevance to us [Y]
CPDLC in Europe (ATN B1) runs over VDL Mode 2. That is a **31.5 kbit/s raw** channel shared by all aircraft in range, using CSMA access. It is roughly 13 times the old ACARS 2.4 kbit/s [A], but small. This explains provider aborts and congestion (C_EASA_SIB_2019_13, C_EU_2015_310). It is an argument against designs that push large payloads (e.g. audio or images) over the ATC data link.
