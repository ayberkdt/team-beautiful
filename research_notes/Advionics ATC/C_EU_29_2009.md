## Content (original 2009 text) [D-ag]
- Art. 1(3): IFR GAT **above FL 285** in the Annex I Part A airspace, and "From 5 February 2015" also Part B.
- Annex II services:
  - **DLIC**: data link initiation.
  - **ACM**: ATC communications management, i.e. automated transfer of communications.
  - **ACL**: ATC clearances and information, i.e. crew requests and reports, controller clearances and instructions.
  - **AMC**: ATC microphone check, which lets a controller instruct several equipped aircraft on a **blocked frequency** to check their voice equipment.
- Art. 3(2): aircraft with first CofA on or after **1 January 2011** must be equipped. Art. 3(3): older aircraft from **5 February 2015**.
- Art. 3(4) exclusions: ED-100/ED-100A (FANS-type) equipment with CofA before 1 January 2014; CofA before 1 January 1998 and leaving by 31 December 2017; State aircraft; test, delivery or maintenance flights or MEL relief.
- Recital 10: retrofitting FANS 1/A aircraft is "not economically justified".
- Annex IV Part B: **ATN and VDL Mode 2**. Recital 16: ATN/VDL Mode 2 is described as the only validated solution. Annex IV Part C allows other protocols if they meet Part D.
- Art. 15: application from **7 February 2013**.
- Recital 1: data link is to "be supplemented by air-ground data link communications", i.e. it adds to voice.

## Relevance to us [Y]
This shows where CPDLC in Europe comes from. It is for the **en-route** phase above FL 285, and it is built to **supplement** voice. It already includes a stuck-mic remedy (the AMC service), so a "check stuck microphone" feature has an existing regulatory precedent.
