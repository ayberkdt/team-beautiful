## Content
- Skies, read via WebFetch summary [D-ag]: DragonFly tested automated emergency diversion (it "communicated with ATC and the operations center" in a simulated incapacitated-crew case), taxi assistance at Toulouse-Blagnac (audio obstacle alerts, speed control, airport-map runway guidance), and a "constant channel of communication" with ATC and the airline OCC. **The article does not mention speech recognition or ATIS.**
- The Air Current, search snippet only [A]: ASR on DragonFly was used to transcribe a controller's taxi instructions and overlay them on a cockpit airport display, for situational awareness and runway-incursion risk. It was also used to transcribe the ATIS broadcast.
- No Airbus primary source on the ASR feature was found in this session. No accuracy numbers.
