## What it says [D-ag]
- **Programme drivers for naval IFF:**
  - reliable identification
  - NATO interoperability
  - integration of many mission systems
  - lower programme risk
  - maintainability and upgradeability
- **Problem with flat panels:** "Modern flat panel systems show reduced detection performance at their intersecting azimuths".
- **L-IESA® features:**
  - 360° coverage
  - electronic steering
  - integrated IFF
  - **Link-16** antenna functions
  - electronic stabilisation for vessel motion
- **Validation:** SkyRF® is the drone-based RF validation platform, increasingly used at sea.
- **Euronaval 2026:** Intersoft is exhibiting there.
- **Not in the blog:** no numbers and no navies named. Multipath, Mode 5 specifics and RES are not covered.
