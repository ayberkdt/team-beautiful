## Numbers [D-ag]
- Goals: > 20 % efficiency, **> 40 W/kg specific power**, > 100 Earth-days, altitude 52–62 km.
- Acceptance: < 25 µm corrosion depth and < 2 % power degradation after **96 % H2SO4,
  96 h** immersion.
- Down-selected encapsulants: **Teflon, Parylene F, solar-cell coverglass**.
- Test panel (one cell + coverglass on honeycomb sandwich, Parylene-coated): **5 % power
  loss from the coating**, then **no corrosion and no detectable performance loss** after
  96 h in 96 % H2SO4; control repeated within 1 %.

## Caveats
Single-cell coupon, immersion at (unstated, presumably room) temperature, 96 h only — far
shorter than a months-long mission; aerosol deposition + thermal cycling not tested.
