## Related, secondary only [A]
- Fluorotherm newsletter (Mar 2009): FEP and PFA chemical resistance "similar to PTFE";
  heat exchangers operating in hot H2SO4 [D-ag qualitative only].
- Search snippets (not verified): PTFE in 98 % H2SO4 at 80 °C 30 d weight change < 0.05 %;
  FEP heat-shrink chart rating "1 = no damage after 30 days" for 98 % H2SO4.
- PTFE/FEP/PFA: I did not obtain an official Chemours chart this session.

## Caveats
168 h only; no UV, no thermal cycling, no permeation data (acid permeation through thin FEP
films over months is the realistic failure mode for coated metals/electronics).
