## Numbers
- Target Venus altitude band **52–62 km**; ≥1 month (stretch 100 Earth-days) [D-ag, poster FY24];
  paper says goal 117 Earth-days [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) §1.1].
- Cloud-layer temperature quoted as roughly 60 °C to −20 °C over the band [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) §1.2].
- Venus acid quoted as >94 % H2SO4 [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) Conclusion].
- **Outer zero-pressure (ZP) envelope: Teflon FEP / Kapton bilaminate**, two metallization
  layers + adhesive; silver second-surface mirror, **α = 0.08, ε = 0.52** [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) §2.1].
- **Inner super-pressure (SP) reservoir: Vectran fabric** (200 denier, 2×2 basket weave,
  76 threads/inch) + heat-sealed thermoplastic urethane bladder; proof 14 kPa, identical unit
  taken to 61 kPa [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) §2.1].
- He permeability of similar metallized material: < 5 cm³/(m²·day) at room temp [D-ag via WebFetch].
- Prototype masses: gondola 7.0 kg, balloon system 21.3 kg, buoyancy control module 19.5 kg;
  Venus mission gondola 100 kg [D-ag via WebFetch, (1) §2.2.1, §5].
- **Acid test: ZP material & seam exposed to 96.2 % H2SO4 at 100 °C under 63 N tension**
  (poster FY23 & FY24 Fig. 2 captions) [D-ag]. Test triad shown: tension at 50 % yield
  strength, 96 % acid, 100 °C oven [D-ag, FY24 figure labels]. Duration not stated.
- FY22: external heat-sealed seams "passed elevated-temperature acid immersion testing" [D-ag].
- SP seam target strength 80 N/mm; improved seam +80 % (design used +30 %) [D-ag, FY24].
- FY24 build: 6.4–6.5 m ZP envelope with 3.5 m SP balloon, SP proof-loaded to 1 psi [D-ag, FY24].
- Gondola design points 25–230 kg [D-ag, FY24].
- 2007 design (Hall et al.): 5.5 m He super-pressure balloon, 44 kg payload, ≥6 days at ~55 km;
  layers outer→inner: **PTFE (acid barrier) / aluminium (solar reflector) / aluminized PET (He
  barrier) / aromatic polyester fabric (Vectran-type) / polyurethane seam coat** [D-ag, tech brief].

## Navigation/comm on the Earth test flights (not Venus) [D-ag via WebFetch]
μINS with dual GPS (1 m baseline); 902 MHz pointable link primary (more reliable than
Iridium); 1 Hz telemetry.
