## Numbers
- Ballistic coefficient **< 50 Pa** ("ultra-low"); deploys **before** atmospheric entry and enters **without an aeroshell** (an implementation of the "LEAF" concept); lifetime "months to a year" [A — AGU abstract, via fetch summary].
- VAPS: VAMP inflated in space (from Venus orbit — needs propulsion to reduce entry speed), ballistic coefficient "two orders of magnitude lower than the rigid aeroshell"; rises to 60 km by day (solar), sinks to 50 km by night; entry/deployment maturity **low**; largest arrival and flying mass of the five VAPS concepts [D-ag, B_vaps2018].
- Wingspan/mass design points circulating in press: 55 m / 900 kg (full), 46 m or 30 m / 450 kg (medium), 6 m / 90 kg (small) — **[A, secondary press/Wikipedia; not verified in a primary]**.

## Relevance
VAMP is the only studied Venus heavier-than-air(-ish) vehicle that avoids aeroshell deployment entirely by being its own entry vehicle. For a rotorcraft this is the "integrated entry body" branch (compare NASA Ames lifting-body autogyro in B_young2026_venus_aerial).
