## Numbers [D-ag]
- Entry: 11.33 km/s, EFPA −10°, interface 180 km; 45° sphere-cone probe, max diameter 1.5 m.
- Orbiter: ~6-h period, 1000 km periapsis, low retrograde inclination (~170°); VOI ~2400 m/s.
- Balloon cycles 48–60 km; circumnavigates Venus in ~6 days (assumed).
- **Relay geometry: ~16 contacts per circumnavigation, each ~8–10 min.**
- **DTE 100 bps; 14-day data volume ≈ 250 MB via orbiter, ≈ 25 MB DTE** (uncompressed).
- Antenna: **crossed dipole, 2.4 GHz S-band**, gondola top; balloon blocks ~10° around zenith;
  usable above ~30° elevation.
- Power: LiCFx primary battery **450 Wh/kg** + secondary; body-mounted solar ~12 W daytime
  average; power subsystem ~5 kg. Gondola: comm 4 kg CBE, thermal 2 kg, structure 3 kg.
- Passive thermal control judged sufficient for 48–60 km cycling.
