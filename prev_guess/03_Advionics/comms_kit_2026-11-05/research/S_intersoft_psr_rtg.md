- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (WebFetch)

## What the page says [D-ag]
- **How it works:** the Radar Target Generator (RTG) detects the radar pulse, applies a fixed and highly precise delay, and retransmits the pulse. The retransmitted pulse has adjusted power, pulse width, frequency and **Doppler shift**.
- **What it can simulate:**
  - programmable RCS, which lets it **simulate drones or stealth aircraft**
  - speed (up to hypersonic) and heading
- **Scenario content:**
  - ground and sea clutter
  - windfarm interference
  - EW jamming
  - 4G/5G interference
  - weather
  - ballistic trajectories (with the "Towertrack" accessories)
- **Combined testing:** when synchronised with RES®, it supports combined PSR + Mode-S/Mode-5 scenarios.
- **What it evaluates:**
  - **Pd**
  - **Pfa**
  - range and azimuth **accuracy**
  - range and azimuth **resolution**
  - **plot latency**
  - **plot load capacity**
- **Not on this page:** numbers. The catalogue gives them: RTG1002 covers L/S band 1001–3450 MHz, range from 36 m to 701 km, and max speed 8190 m/s (see S_intersoft_rass_catalogue.md).
- **Customers:** none named.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- The list of PSR acceptance metrics the jury uses is **Pd, Pfa, accuracy, resolution, latency and load**.
- A non-cooperative (primary radar) approach-guidance concept should quote targets for each of these metrics.
- It should also propose RTG injection with drone-RCS targets as a test.
