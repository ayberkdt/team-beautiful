## Content (publication page) [D-ag]
- **12** VDL2 frequencies are available. **6** were in operational use in 2024, with a 7th expected by 2025, so about half are in use.
- Projections for 2028 and 2035. VDL2 "can remain viable until 2035" with targeted enhancements.
- Based on 2024 operational data. The insights are meant to inform improvements from Summer 2026.
- The page gives no provider-abort rates, message counts or latency figures. These would need the PDF.

## Relevance to us [Y]
The VDL2 capacity ceiling is a planning fact: Europe expects VDL2 to last until about 2035, with SATCOM (Iris, see C_EASA_ESSP_Iris_2023) as the next link. If someone needs PA rates or latency, the next step is the 18.7 MB PDF, which should be read before quoting any such figure.
