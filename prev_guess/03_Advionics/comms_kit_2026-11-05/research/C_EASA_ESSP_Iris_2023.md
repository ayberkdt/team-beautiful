# C_EASA_ESSP_Iris_2023: "Certification of ESSP as pan-European data link provider via SATCOM"

- **Issuing body:** EASA (newsroom)
- **URL:** https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/newsroom-and-events/news/certification-essp-pan-european-data-link-provider-satcom
- **Date:** 25 Jul 2023
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04

## Content [D-ag]
- ESSP SAS received initial certification as a pan-European Communication Services Provider for the **IRIS** service, "the first pan-European certified service provider for datalink services".
- IRIS is an AMS(R)S satellite datalink supporting **ATN-B1/ATS-B2 CPDLC** and ATS-B2 ADS-C.
- It uses the **Inmarsat geostationary** constellation. Consortium: ESSP SAS, Inmarsat and SITA. NewPENS and Collins Aerospace were expected to join.

## Relevance to us [Y]
SATCOM data link for European ATC is certified and real. It is the long-term alternative to congested VDL2 and works where VHF line of sight fails. It is still a data link, though, with the same "not time-critical" limits.
