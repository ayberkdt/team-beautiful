# S_intersoft_bhansa_case: Customer case "ANSP Enhances CNS Measurement Capabilities with SkyRF® Drone" (BHANSA)

- **URL:** https://intersoft-electronics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Customer-case-BHANSA-SkyRF-1.pdf
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04. WebFetch saved the PDF and I read the text with pdftotext.

## What it says [D-ag]
- **Customer:** BHANSA (Bosnia and Herzegovina Air Navigation Services Agency) adopted SkyRF®. The system was developed by Intersoft Electronics and Skyguide.
- **What BHANSA wanted:**
  - higher measurement accuracy
  - operational efficiency
  - lower environmental impact
- **Training:** theory plus practical measurements on:
  - VOR/DME
  - ILS
  - radar systems
  - Possible extensions discussed: advanced radar parameters, and **PAPI and ALS** (approach lighting) verification.
- **Results claimed:**
  - "CNS parameters captured in a single 10-minute flight"
  - automated drone paths "outperform traditional flight checks" in repeatability
  - "Up to 50% fewer test flights"
  - altitude measurements "reduce ground clutter"
- **Quote:** an unnamed BHANSA representative praises the training.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- Confirms a **named ANSP customer** (BHANSA) and the Skyguide partnership.
- Shows approach-lighting systems (PAPI/ALS) are inside the group's verification scope.
