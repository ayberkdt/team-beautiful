## What the page says [D-ag]
- NORA® is a family of non-rotating arrays "for S-Band and L-Band primary radar and for SSR/IFF systems", for civil and military use.
- It uses a **circular array** rather than multi-faced planar arrays. Electronic steering "maintains near equal performance at all scan angles".
- Power amplifiers sit close to the radiating elements.
- **Scan modes:** continuous rotation, confirmation look-backs, and sector scanning.
- **Beams:** dual surveillance beams (longer dwell or faster update), combined surveillance and tracking beams, and customizable beam patterns.
- **Claimed benefits:** no rotary joints or bearings, lower maintenance, redundancy, lower power, lighter weight.
- **Gaps:** the page gives no numeric specs and names no customers.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- NORA's **sector scanning** and **dedicated tracking beam** could give a much higher update rate on a single aircraft on final approach.
- That is the technical hook for a "radar-based approach monitoring/guidance" concept built on the sponsor's own antenna technology.
