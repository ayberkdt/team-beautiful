## Content [D-ag]
- Applies from **"May 1st, 2025"**. The change was due in the eAIP Belgium & Luxembourg (ENR 1.1 §1.10.5) from 15 May 2025.
- "SERA.14083 is not a literal transposition of the ICAO provisions".
- Where ICAO splits RCF procedures into VMC and IMC, SERA splits them into **VFR and IFR** flights.
- For IFR flights on a SID/STAR, any RCF procedure published on the chart takes precedence over the general IFR procedure.
- Standard IFR RCF: maintain the last assigned speed and level/altitude for **20 minutes**. "Unlike in ICAO Annex 2, these 20 minutes also apply to IFR flights operating in airspace where radar is used".
- The new SSR code **7601** is for an IFR flight in VMC whose pilot-in-command decides to continue in VMC and land at the nearest suitable aerodrome.
- Decision chart: RCF → VFR → **7600**. RCF → IFR → no diversion → **7600**. RCF → IFR → continue in VMC and divert to the nearest suitable aerodrome → **7601**.
- Further reading: the consolidated 923/2012 effective 01/05/2025 (CELEX 02012R0923-20240501) and the EASA Easy Access Rules for SERA.

## Relevance to us [Y]
This is a short, authoritative confirmation from a national authority that (a) the EU timer is 20 min in both radar and non-radar airspace, whereas ICAO Annex 2 uses 7 min where radar is used, and (b) 7601 is the EU-only "IFR in VMC, diverting" code. A pan-European cockpit aid must hold an EU rule set that is separate from the ICAO default. The leaflet also notes that Switzerland and the UK differ (see C_OpsGroup_SERA_2025).
