# S_esut_wilhelmshaven_2022: ESUT.de "Intersoft Electronics Services in Wilhelmshaven gegründet" (7 Sep 2022)

- **URL:** https://esut.de/en/2022/09/meldungen/36577/intersoft-electronics-services-in-wilhelmshaven-gegruendet/
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (WebFetch). This is trade press; the article credits Intersoft as its source.

## What it says [D-ag]
- **New company:** Intersoft Electronics Group founded **Intersoft Electronics Services GmbH** in Wilhelmshaven to serve Germany, Austria and Switzerland (D-A-CH).
- **Trigger:** a **Saab order to support integration of L-IESA Non-Rotating IFF into the German Navy's F123 frigates**.
- **Existing customers named:**
  - German Navy
  - Luftwaffe
  - RUAG
  - **Deutsche Flugsicherung (DFS)**
  - **Skyguide**
  - **Austrocontrol**
- **Jobs:** a "double-digit" number of jobs is planned; no exact figure is given.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- This is the best public evidence of **who uses the group's products**:
  - **L-IESA on the German F123 frigates, via Saab**;
  - civil ANSP customers DFS, Skyguide and Austrocontrol.
