# S_canso_sms_1M_hours: CANSO news "Intersoft Electronics' Surveillance Monitoring System surpasses 1 million operational hours" (26 Nov 2024)

- **URL:** https://canso.org/intersoft-electronics-surveillance-monitoring-system-surpasses-1-million-operational-hours/
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (WebFetch)

## What it says [D-ag]
- **Milestone:** in October 2024 the SMS passed **1,000,000 operational hours**.
- **Deployment:** in Europe and Asia Pacific since **2011**.
- **What SMS does:** monitors and reports surveillance parameters in real time against manufacturer and regulatory specifications (ICAO, EUROCONTROL, FAA).
- **Features:**
  - automated alarms when a value goes out of tolerance;
  - trend reports for proactive maintenance.
- **What the article does not give:** the number of systems, countries, customers, or any quotes.
