# C_EUROCONTROL_CSS_service: Call sign similarity service (CSS)

- **Issuing body:** EUROCONTROL Network Manager, Call Sign Management Cell (CSMC) in the NM Operations Centre
- **URL:** https://www.eurocontrol.int/service/call-sign-similarity-service
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04 (no page date shown)

## Content [D-ag]
- Aim: "We help to reduce the incidence of call sign confusion events." Call sign similarity means aircraft in the same area, on the same frequency, with similar call signs.
- Service levels: **Level 0** public awareness and CSS rules; **Level 1** de-confliction within a single operator's schedule before the summer and winter seasons, using the **Call Sign Similarity Tool (CSST)** (subscription required); **Level 2** between operators is planned and not available.
- Rule types: single-operator rules; local ANSP rules (e.g. limits on alphanumeric call signs); single-call-sign rules (similarity to runway designators, flight levels, VHF frequencies, considered less severe).
- Strict rules would block airline schedules, so CSST parameters are tuned from operational feedback.
- Effectiveness is monitored through EVAIR (see C_EVAIR_Bulletin25). The page gives no statistics.

## Relevance to us [Y]
Call sign confusion is handled **upstream** (schedule planning). A cockpit system can only add a last line of defence: flag when a received call sign is close to own-ship but not identical, or when a number in a call sign could be misheard as a flight level, heading or frequency (EUROCONTROL treats this as a known similarity class).
