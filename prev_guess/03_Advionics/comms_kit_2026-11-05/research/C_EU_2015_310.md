# C_EU_2015_310: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2015/310, amending 29/2009 (data link) and repealing 441/2014

- **Issuing body:** European Commission. Read on legislation.gov.uk "as adopted".
- **URL:** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2015/310/adopted/data.htm?view=plain
- **Adopted:** 26 February 2015
- **Date read:** 2026-11-04

## Content [D-ag]
- Recital 2: ANSPs and operators reported **"Provider Aborts" (PAs)**, i.e. disconnections of air-ground data communications beyond acceptable performance levels. Some ANSPs limited service to aircraft on "white lists".
- Recital 3: an EASA investigation found the random PAs had no single predictable cause and pointed to the RF environment and the **single-frequency** infrastructure.
- Recital 4: acceptable performance requires a **multi-frequency** infrastructure.
- Recitals 6-7: SESAR remedial work was expected around 2016 to 2018, so the dates were deferred.
- Art. 1(2) (amending Art. 3(2)): operators must have the capability **"as from 5 February 2020"**.
- Art. 1(3) (amending Art. 15): "7 February 2013" is replaced by **"5 February 2018"**.
- Recital 9: ground infrastructure must be available **24 months** before the airborne date.
- New exemptions (Art. 3(3)):
  - (a) CofA before **1 January 2014** with Eurocae-listed equipment;
  - (b) CofA before **31 December 2003** and ceasing operation before **31 December 2022**;
  - (c) State aircraft;
  - (d) test, delivery or maintenance flights or MEL relief.

## Relevance to us [Y]
This is official evidence that the European ATC data link was unreliable enough (provider aborts) to delay the mandate by about 5 years. For the pitch: data link can drop silently, so any system that relies on it needs a voice fallback and must show link status. It is also the reason why "datalink replaces voice on approach" is not a credible claim today.
