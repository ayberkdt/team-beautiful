# R_audit_2026-11-04 — independent spot audit of cited values in doc 24 (Advionics comms/ATC)

Auditor: independent audit subagent, 4 Nov 2026. Scope: 12 values from `03_Advionics/docs/24_advionics_comms_atc.md`, each checked
against the source cited for it (URLs from `src/aerokit/references/_task_atc.py` and the agent notes in this folder).

## Method

1. **Selection (stratified, seeded random).** I listed 30 candidate values by topic (lost comm, VHF/8.33 kHz, Mode S /
   ADS-B, EUROCONTROL radar/ESASSP/ARTAS, ASTERIX, ATC ASR, PAR/ILS, sponsor), each with its dossier line and tag.
   Script `D:\tmp_ideathon\advionics\audit\scripts\pick.py` (seed 20261104) drew one value per topic, then topped up
   to 12 so that there are at least 4 [D] and 6 [D-ag]. Output kept in `D:\tmp_ideathon\advionics\audit\pick_output.txt`.
   The stratified draw gave 5 [D] and 3 [D-ag], so 4 more [D-ag] were drawn. Final set: **5 [D], 7 [D-ag]**.
   Note: for "EUROCONTROL radar/ESASSP" the draw landed on the ARTAS line (L169), not on the 1997 standard or ESASSP;
   I kept the draw as it came out rather than choose by hand.
2. **Reading.** HTML pages: WebFetch (a summarising fetch tool; I asked for verbatim quotes, but a summariser
   sits between me and the page — so for HTML rows the quote is as relayed by that tool). PDFs: WebFetch saved them,
   I copied them to `D:\tmp_ideathon\advionics\audit\pdf\` and extracted the text with PyMuPDF
   (`scripts\extract.py`, run with `python -I`) into `D:\tmp_ideathon\advionics\audit\txt\`. Those text files are the
   evidence for the PDF rows; you can grep them.
3. **Verdicts.** MATCH = the number and its condition are in the source. PARTIAL = the number is right, but the
   context, condition or attribution in the dossier differs from the source, or a qualifier is missing. MISMATCH =
   the source says something else. UNREACHABLE = the source could not be read.

## Corrections needed (author to apply)

1. **#1, L154 (and wherever "as VFR" is repeated):** replace "7601 = IFR continuing in VMC as VFR to nearest
   aerodrome" with "7601 = IFR flight that encounters VMC and whose pilot-in-command decides to continue in VMC: land
   at the nearest suitable aerodrome, report arrival (SERA.14083(c)(6))". The rule does not say the flight becomes VFR.
2. **#5, L169** (L279, "ARTAS … uses an IMM tracker", is fine as it stands): replace "ARTAS: IMM tracker (uniform motion, speed change, turns), JPDA" with "ARTAS:
   tracking architecture largely based on an IMM-inspired design (Blom et al.; uniform motion / speed change /
   left-right turn were the modes of the NLR IMM research that led to ARTAS); PDA later replaced by JPDA". Keep
   "close to 90 % of European daily flights at 43 ACCs" and cite it to the EUROCONTROL ARTAS page.
3. **#11, L180:** split the line. Tower study (Cardosi 1994, DOT/FAA/RD-94/15): < 1 % readback errors, 7 of 19
   unnoticed by controllers, miscommunication < 1 % for 1–5 elements and 1.4 % / 1.3 % for 6 / 7 elements.
   En-route (Cardosi 1993, as cited in the 1994 report): 1–3 % for 1–4 elements, **8 % for five or more**.
4. **#12, L166 and §0 card L29:** add the fixed terms: "accuracy EL 6 m from course / 0.4 % of range + 10 % of
   offset, AZ 9 m / 0.6 % + 10 %, range 30 m / 3 % of range". The ENRI slide writes them with a "/". It does not say
   which one applies. I have not checked how Annex 10 combines them (my unverified recollection is "whichever is
   greater"; Annex 10 was not read). Consequence for the code: `src/aerokit/atc/par.py` lines 61–62 use only the
   percentage terms (`0.004 * rng_m + 0.1 * abs(ev)`, `0.006 * rng_m + 0.1 * abs(lv)`). Near touchdown that gives
   much smaller errors than the 6 m / 9 m figures. The author should decide whether to add the fixed term to the
   error model.
5. **Registry, `etsi_en300676_1` (side finding):** year 2016 → 2011 (V1.5.2, 2011-03, per the PDF cover).
