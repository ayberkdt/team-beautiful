## What the page says [D-ag]
- **Engineering disciplines:**
  - systems engineering: architecture and requirements
  - software: embedded software and hardware/software integration
  - hardware: digital, mixed-signal and RF electronics, PCB, subsystem integration
  - mechanical: enclosures and 3D CAD
  - V&V: functional, environmental and stress testing, plus calibration
- **Processing:** "FPGAs and CPLDs" for "high-speed, deterministic processing for real-time applications".
- **Frequency range:** "5 MHz to 26 GHz" for systems, and "DC to 26 GHz" for testing.
- **Applications:** radar, satcom, TV and CATV reception, healthcare, and naval IFF including L-IESA®.
- **Certifications:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and EN 9100.
- **Location:** the engineering team sits on the production site.
- **Overlap with Advionics:** the text closely matches the Advionics product-development page (S_advionics_product_development.md). This shared text points to one shared engineering and manufacturing organisation for both companies.
- **Not mentioned:** no programming languages and no signal-processing toolchains.

## Relevance to us [Y]
- The group's engineering stack is RF + FPGA + embedded + mechanical, with qualification to military and avionics standards.
- Concepts that put **deterministic real-time processing (FPGA)** at the sensor edge will fit their engineering culture.
