## What it says [D-ag]
The kit is a lightweight toolbox for periodic checks of PSR and SSR radars. It works with any radar manufacturer and is used "with minimal operational impact".

| Module | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Radar Gyroscope and Inclinometer | Antenna support and tower behaviour under wind and temperature; platform levelling; encoder quality |
| USB Power Meter | Tx and Tx-driver power; pulse amplitude, shape and timing (width, rise and fall times, timed sidelobes) |
| Digital RF Analyzer | Antenna and receiver checks on PSR and SSR: uplink recordings give the transmit antenna diagram; receiver dynamic range, noise level, bandwidth and alignment; calibration |
| USB Video Recorder | Downlink recordings; the PSR horizontal polar diagram (HPD) on receive; **solar recording to build the PSR vertical polar diagram (VPD)**; sensitivity of the whole system including the antenna chain |
| Radar Timing Interface | Captures and distributes the antenna timing signals (ACP/ARP) |

- The leaflet's tagline is "Monitor, Measure, Service your Sensors".
- **The leaflet gives no numeric specifications.**
