## SR 2024-004 — "ensure the canopies fitted to all Extra NG aircraft are manufactured to meet the required certification standards and can withstand expected aerodynamic and flight loads"

- Made 16 Feb 2024. EASA response received 26 Apr 2024:
  - The bonding process is unchanged from the earlier EA 300 type, which had no similar in-service occurrence.
  - The TC holder's conservative CFD analysis shows the canopy assembly meets the certification standards.
- Update of 29 Jan 2025:
  - EASA is evaluating with the manufacturer a **voluntary one-time NDT** to verify the bonding of in-service canopy frames.
  - EASA is **considering the BFU scenario**: that the change in the locking mechanism of the Extra NG canopy, compared with
    the previous models, might have played a role.
- Status **Open**; AAIB assessment **Partially Adequate**; planned action ongoing; update due **1 May 2025**.
- Outcome after May 2025: **not found** (R1 also found no later update).

## SR 2024-005 — "assess the effectiveness of SB-NG-2-22 in rectifying inadequate bonding"

- Made 16 Feb 2024. EASA response received 28 Nov 2024:
  - The AAIB's conclusion on the SB's scope is correct.
  - EASA nevertheless considers the canopies compliant without the SB.
  - The manufacturer will recommend a **one-off canopy-frame inspection per AMM 20-10-06**.
  - "not overlooking the possibility that the canopy was not correctly latched", the manufacturer offers an **improvement on
    the latching system** with proactive outer-frame replacement or a new canopy.
- EASA status **Closed — Partial Agreement**; AAIB assessment **Partially Adequate**. The AAIB says the response does not show
  the foam will spread over all surfaces or that the surfaces are properly prepared.

## Use in the work [Y]
